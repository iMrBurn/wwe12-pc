## Android (new in 1.0)

**`WWE12-PC-Android-v1.0.0.apk`** is the Android version of WWE '12 PC (ARM64, Android 9 or newer). **No game files are included**: you need your own copy of WWE '12 (Xbox 360), as an ISO or a game folder. Open the app, tap **CHOOSE MY ISO**, then **PLAY**.

- It has the same game as the PC 1.0: wide screens (matches fill 20:9 phones), 360p/480p, the bigger WWE Universe, the Match Creator extras, real move-set names, the jukebox and faster loading. In the app: new resolutions, "Wide screens" switch, WWE UNIVERSE options and **ADD DLC...**.
- **Update from an Android alpha (1 to 8):** download the APK by hand and install it over your alpha (same signing key: your saves are kept). From now on the app finds new versions by itself in the v1.x.x releases.
- **Tested** on an Android emulator only (menus, a 20:9 screen, 6 match loads in a row without a freeze). Real phones: only the alphas were tested by players.
- **Works best** on Snapdragon (Adreno) 7xx/8xx phones with 6 GB of memory or more. Mali (MediaTek, Exynos, Tensor) phones should show colour but are not confirmed yet.
- **Known problems:** many Snapdragon 845/855/870 (Adreno 6xx) phones cannot start the game with their own driver (it lacks Vulkan features the game needs): the app says so, and a recent Turnip driver may help (#41, #43, #44). Instant replay stays off by default; some freezes are still investigated (#46, #47, #49, #51). The phone gets warm: lower the resolution or turn off crowd and shadows.
- Not done yet: PlayStation button icons, a Mod Maker for Android, and checks on real phones. Send the log (launcher > **SHARE LAST LOG**) with your phone model if something goes wrong.
